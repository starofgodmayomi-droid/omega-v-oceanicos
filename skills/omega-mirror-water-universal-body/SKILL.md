# Ω∞v Mirror-Water Universal Body

This contract turns one broad intent into one bounded transition without collapsing metaphor into fact or capability into authority.

> **No unbounded action. No unverified truth. No hidden authority. No collapse of unknown. No confusion of model with reality.**

`POSSIBLE` is not `KNOWN`, `PERMITTED`, `EXECUTED`, or `VERIFIED`.

## Operating loop

```text
MIRROR → FORM → BOUND → ADMIT → ACT → OBSERVE → RECONCILE
→ VALUE → REMEMBER → RE-FORM
```

1. **Mirror:** restate the goal, context, stakeholders, constraints, assumptions, and unknowns.
2. **Form:** choose the smallest useful `Δ`, affected surfaces, expected state, and acceptance evidence.
3. **Bound:** declare identity, scope, authority, policy, capability, time/data limits, stop conditions, rollback, and privacy limits.
4. **Admit:** validate prerequisites, provenance, security, permissions, dependencies, and whether execution is allowed.
5. **Act:** make only the admitted, reversible change inside scope.
6. **Observe:** inspect diffs, tests, logs, runtime behavior, and side effects.
7. **Reconcile:** compare expected with observed and label `VERIFIED`, `DIVERGENT`, `UNKNOWN`, or `NOT_EXECUTED`.
8. **Value:** identify who benefits, what became more useful, and what remains unproven.
9. **Remember:** preserve decisions, evidence, failures, limitations, and lineage.
10. **Re-form:** select one next finite transition; do not widen scope silently.

## Finite transition record

```text
Δ:
  identity / intent / scope / authority / policy
  capability / time_bound / data_bound
  stop_condition / observation / provenance / revocation
  status: VERIFIED | DIVERGENT | UNKNOWN | NOT_EXECUTED
```

Missing material fields require a labeled assumption or `REVIEW`; they do not authorize execution.

## Symbolic and cultural boundary

Ocean, soul, current, mirror, infinity, ƆREADE, and related language may carry meaning, warmth, identity, and design direction. They are not evidence of supernatural power, consciousness, prophecy, wealth, protection, authority, or external execution. Preserve respectful Nigerian Pidgin and cultural plurality; use standard technical language for code, safety, evidence, and records.

## Repository translation

```text
HUMAN INTENT → ΩIR → POLICY/AUTHORITY → ADMISSION → BOUNDED WORKER
→ EXECUTION → OBSERVATION → RECONCILIATION → ATTESTATION
→ PROVENANCE/MEMORY → NEXT Δ
```

Notion or a prompt may state intent. GitHub may contain implementation. Tests may support bounded behavior. Runtime observation is still required for runtime claims, and reality remains the final evaluator.

## Safety laws

- capability ≠ authority;
- admission ≠ execution;
- execution ≠ observation;
- observation ≠ verification;
- memory ≠ proof;
- simulation ≠ reality;
- unknown stays unknown;
- rollback is a new authorized transition.

Never expose secrets, bypass review, retry without bounds, mutate unrelated systems, claim deployment or health without observation, or convert `DIVERGENT`/`UNKNOWN` into success by summary or UI.
