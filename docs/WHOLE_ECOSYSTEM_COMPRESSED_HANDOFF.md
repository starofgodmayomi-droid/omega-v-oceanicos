## Authority and epistemic boundaries

- Reality and accepted external observation are the final authority; a prompt, memory, plan, model, test, CI run, signature, dashboard, or attestation is not reality itself.
- Notion = intent, meaning, design, and context. GitHub = versioned implementation, review, and CI. Runtime = execution and observation. Reconciliation compares expected and observed state. Memory preserves provenance and correction lineage.
- Preserve `UNKNOWN`, `DIVERGENT`, `NOT_EXECUTED`, and `MODEL_ONLY`; do not silently promote them.
- `POSSIBLE ≠ KNOWN ≠ REPRESENTABLE ≠ PERMITTED ≠ PROPOSED ≠ ATTEMPTED ≠ EXECUTED ≠ OBSERVED ≠ VERIFIED ≠ ATTESTED ≠ DEPLOYED ≠ HEALTHY`.
- `MODEL OUTPUT ≠ CLAIM`; `CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY`; `PROPOSAL ≠ ACTION`; `MEMORY ≠ PROOF`; `CI ≠ REALITY`; `SIMULATION ≠ REALITY`.
- Least privilege, explicit authorization, bounded work, finite steps, stop conditions, revocation, provenance, and fail-closed behavior remain invariants. No hidden network, arbitrary shell, or undeclared capability is implied.
